# Weather and camera-underwater environment

[Scope and pins](README.md). These are missing environment inputs/consumers, not a claim that ordinary authored sky, liquid rendering or day/night lighting is absent. Depth darkening is separately classified as reference uncertainty, not a required effect inferred from an isolated assignment.

## ENV1: Weather state has no precipitation or storm-light consumer

**Reference.** `W/WoW/Source/Ui/GameUI.cpp:6619–6637` supplies weather state, including `WeatherRec::m_transitionSkyBox`. `W/WoW/Source/WorldClient/MapWeather.cpp:719–784` accepts type/intensity and that sky-transition blend input; despite its argument name, the latter is not a caller-supplied duration. `:584–704` updates state and chooses precipitation implementations; `:373–424` derives intensity/fog/density interpolation from elapsed time and state deltas. `:564–580` renders active rain/snow/sand. `W/WoW/Source/WorldClient/World.cpp:1570,4885–4887` reaches update/render. `W/WoW/Source/WorldClient/Rain.cpp:39–85` drives rain/mist rate and motion-blur orientation; player/transport motion history comes from `MapWeather.cpp:789–875`. Weather intensity reaches storm light sampling/blending at `W/WoW/Source/WorldClient/DayNight.cpp:2630–2639,2803–2830`.

**Current.** Native environmental updates at `E/godot/rust/src/lib.rs:2643–2670` pass map, player position, world time and WMO fog into `WorldLighting::sync`. That consumer at `E/godot/rust/src/lighting/mod.rs:190–244` has no weather type/intensity/blend input. The ordinary Retail catalog explicitly samples `LightParamsSlot::Clear` (`E/godot/rust/src/lighting/assets.rs:146–152`), not a weather-driven clear/storm blend. Native world/render source has no corresponding precipitation lifecycle. M2 emitters and animated water are not weather-state consumers.

**Required change.** Produce and consume weather state for the intended contract: precipitation type, density/intensity, source-defined interpolation, player/transport-motion response and relevant storm-light/sky changes. Keep existing clear-sky/day-night behavior. Do not turn `transitionSkyBox` into a duration or infer authoritative server scheduling/new wire fields from this historical client.

**Acceptance.** Feed reference-compatible rain/snow inputs with known initial state, intensity 0.5 and a known sky-transition blend value. At explicit elapsed-time checkpoints, compare state with `MapWeather.cpp:373–424`; a settled 0.5 intensity reaches the precipitation renderer at approximately 0.3333 after its threshold/remap, not 0.5. Verify type, nonzero density and storm-light selection, then clear-weather interpolation/fade-out. Hold camera input fixed while varying player/transport history to isolate motion response; camera velocity alone is not the reference wind input. No map-weather, complete-family or current-server-policy claim is made.

## ENV2: Camera immersion does not select underwater scene fog and light

**Reference.** `W/WoW/Source/WorldClient/World.cpp:1880–1896` produces camera liquid identity and depth; `:750–756` exposes the state. `W/WoW/Source/WorldClient/DayNight.cpp:2619–2639` uses liquid identity to select underwater light parameters. `:2143–2189,2206–2236` chooses/blends underwater versus interior fog with liquid/fog flags. These are actual camera-context selection paths, distinct from the uncertain depth-darkening assignments described in [V5](validation-candidates.md#v5-camera-depth-darkening-has-reference-sign-and-ordering-conflicts).

**Current.** `E/godot/rust/src/lib.rs:2643–2670` queries camera WMO fog but no camera liquid state. `E/godot/rust/src/lighting/mod.rs:190–244,477–503` combines the ordinary catalog fog with WMO ordinary fog, not immersion-selected scene fog. `E/godot/rust/src/lighting/assets.rs:161–173` samples underwater data into **water material** inputs. `E/godot/shaders/water.gdshader:183–206` fogs refracted scene color according to surface-to-scene thickness. That real water-surface effect does not switch scene-wide lighting/fog for terrain, models and sky when the camera is submerged.

**Required change.** Derive camera liquid context and use the applicable underwater scene-light/fog selection and WMO/liquid flag precedence. Preserve surface refraction/thickness separately. Do not key this solely off player swimming: submerged camera and player feet are different observations. Do not require depth-dependent darkening until the reference sign/update-order ambiguity is resolved.

**Acceptance.** Keep map, time, ordinary light, WMO fog and player position fixed; move only the camera across a known liquid surface with distinct ordinary/underwater light and fog inputs. Verify selected scene parameters and terrain/model pixels even without a water-surface fragment in front of them. Test liquid flags that restore group fog and a no-liquid control. Depth telemetry must follow the producer's `level - camera_z` convention; physical immersion does not by itself establish a darkening ramp. Asset relevance, native pixels, liquid-depth glare and underwater audio remain unproven.

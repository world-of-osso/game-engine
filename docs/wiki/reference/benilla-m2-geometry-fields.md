## Animation tracks and lookup tables feeding materials

Shared descriptor: disk AnimBlock has interpolation u16, global-sequence i16, outer timestamp-array descriptor, and outer value-array descriptor; inner per-sequence arrays contain timestamps and typed values. This descriptor representation is shared context for rows below, not a unique field expansion per every track instance. Exact E parser offsets/decoding: `godot/core/src/asset/m2_format/m2_anim.rs:295–382`; E retained `AnimTrack { interpolation_type, global_sequence, sequences }` at `:201-230`. E material sampler treats interpolation1 as linear and all other values as step, chooses local/global time (`godot/core/src/m2_material.rs:279-325`). B v256 track layout is different: interp/gseq, per-sequence ranges, inner absolute-time keys; `crates/benilla-m2/src/track.rs:5-22,50-89`. B’s track semantics/offsets must not be imported into E.

## Explicit bounds, opaque spans, and gaps

- The E `RawM2Submesh` declaration expands to 48 bytes, including fields that may exceed legacy 32-byte sections. E’s `parse_submeshes` currently calls generic `parse_binrw_entries::<RawM2Submesh>` with `size_of::<T>()` stride and has no visible version-dependent stride gate (`godot/core/src/asset/m2_format/parser.rs:144-172,269-283`). Exact supported E SKIN version/stride contract was not independently proven in this bounded source pass; asset/version applicability remains unvalidated; parser record width itself is48 bytes.
- E standalone SKIN parser does not decode bone-indices array payload; retains resolved regular index stream, decoded submeshes and batches; raw SkinData also carries the lookup and selector vectors before model assembly. B M2View header bone-count and properties array are skipped/unretained. These are known opaque/dropped regions, not claims about downstream B elsewhere.
- E modern and B vanilla record names can appear similar while occupying distinct offsets/roles: E splits flags+priority bytes, B stores u16 flags at byte 0; E byte+12 material_layer versus an unread B word at+12; both parsers read texture_count at+14. Exact versioned wire layouts supersede semantic-name guesses.
- Material/color/transparency/UV consumers are traced through core batch/material logic and production Godot material/shader. B-only producer/model facts are traced; its production renderer is outside the bounded `benilla-m2` reader source slice, so B consumer claims are intentionally limited to explicit crate comments/helpers.
- No global absence claims. Search boundaries: E `godot/core/src/asset/m2_format/{parser.rs,m2_anim.rs}`, `godot/core/src/{m2.rs,m2_material.rs,asset/m2_batch_data.rs,asset/m2_texture.rs}`, selected production `godot/rust/src/assets/{mod.rs,material.rs,uv_animation.rs}`, `godot/shaders/m2.gdshader`; B `crates/benilla-m2/src/{lib.rs,model.rs,skin.rs,track.rs}`. Broader callers or other packages may add behavior.

## Falsifications of the draft

The draft’s assertions that B texture lookup is used by `batch_binding`, that E batch flags drive priority sorting, and that E texture transforms are fully resolved by `asset/m2_batch_data.rs` were not supported by the source: B’s M2RawData table is retained but no use was found in bounded B reader files; E priority plane is its own byte field; direct E transform resolution is in `core/src/m2_material.rs:166-184`. Corrected above. The draft’s E claim that submesh stride is version-gated is also unsupported by the inspected parser, which uses `RawM2Submesh`’s declared size for each entry.

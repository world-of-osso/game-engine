## Limits preserved

- **No builds, tests, source execution/imports, clients, downloads, extraction, asset-corpus acceptance, rendered comparison or runtime-parity work.** Proof uses local source/Markdown inspection, bounded text searches and read-only Git metadata. No engine implementation is assigned by a research gap.
- Every comparison is version/source qualified. Benilla's older offsets, flags, coordinate conventions and reader behavior are not transplanted into modern E; incomplete unknown spans remain unnamed.
- Parsed, internally retained, publicly retained, derived, bound material inputs and actual consumers are separate states. Examples: E IMPASS drops at the public terrain adapter; detail shadow use differs from terrain-surface shadow binding; shader UV velocity use does not prove a production terrain clock advances.
- Bounded unknowns are not absence proofs: camera/event payload consumers, modern WMO extensions, ADT blend/LOD/flight inputs, weather counterparts and unrequested CSV/schema fields retain their explicit boundaries. Native debug cloud/flag consumers must not be promoted into ordinary inworld rendering claims.
- Unresolved Sakuin authorization-transport certification and its existing proof remain separate parked context. This inventory neither repeats deployment nor certifies that independent gap.

# Stockade Floor Fall

`game-server-admin teleport <char> 34 x y z` to (97-101, 76-78) or (74, -16) in The Stockade dropped the player through the floor; after the fall the client drew sky instead of the dungeon. Teleports to x 103 and to creature spawn points held. Evidence: `../../../data/diagnostics/stockadefixes-20260926/`.

## Root cause: the points are outside the WMO

- The Stockade is one global WMO (108631, WDT 791060, placed at the origin; WoW = (-x, -y, z) of the file coordinates). A vertical ray through every triangle of its 27 groups, with no MOPY filter, crosses **no face at all** in the columns (97, 76), (99, 77), (101, 78) and (74, -16). They are inside wall and rock between the cells. The Jail18 cell floor begins at x ≈ 102 for y 76-78. At y -16 the Jail02 cell floor spans x 77-92.
- Floor collision itself holds: over a 2 yd grid of the whole dungeon, the server `GroundMap` finds every walkable collidable face that a brute-force scan finds (7,650 of 7,650). The client uses the same `shared::ground` rule. All 93 TDB1210 Stockade spawns stand on a floor.
- Historical WMO-only live (`03`, `04`): teleported to (103, 76, -34.5) the player stayed at -34.9. Teleported to (99, 77, -34.5) the player fell to -1255 within 12 s. Full M2 floor bakes change the first column as described below.

## Full M2 bake: authentic barrel support — verified 2026-10-12

The two regression failures at `(103,76)` were **inside-floor controls**, not the unsupported fall columns. Canonical map34 M2 data correctly resolves the top of `world/generic/passivedoodads/barrel/barrel01.m2` (FDID199563) at `-33.924946`, above the Jail18 WMO floor `-34.85623`.

- WDT791060 MODF selects WMO108631, unique ID `0xffffffff`, position/rotation zero, flags `0x8`, doodad set0, scale1. The root has exactly one MODS, `Set_$DefaultGlobal`, range `[0,748)`: no other set/WMO is involved.
- Barrel MODD index523 references MODI index8, flags byte `0x02`, file position `(-102.728981,-75.935272,-34.853554)`, quaternion `(0,0,-0.321439326,0.946930170)`, scale `0.934959114`. Jail18 group17 (FDID108649) references523 through MODR. Origin placement converts file `(x,y,z)` to WoW `(-x,-y,z)`; the barrel centre is `(102.728981,75.935272,-34.853554)`.
- MD20 global flags are `0x80`, with60 authored collision indices,12 vertices and20 face normals. Triangle16 has upward normal `(0,0,1)` and WoW corners `(102.890944,75.485446,-33.924945)`, `(103.173269,75.853379,-33.924945)`, `(102.995792,76.281846,-33.924945)`. Independent composition/barycentric intersection reproduces the baked height. TrinityCore's extractor admits models by nonempty bounding triangles, without a global-flags collision veto; this is an authored solid barrel, not render geometry substituted for collision.
- Independent scans of all WMO faces and active M2 hulls find no hits at existing fall columns `(97,76)`, `(99,77)`, `(101,78)`, `(74,-16)`. The global-WMO map has no terrain support. Bare floor controls `(106,76)` and `(104.5,77.3)` have WMO height `-34.85623` and no M2 hull intersections.

Tests retain the genuine fall columns, move the bare WMO controls off the barrel, and assert barrel support explicitly on both server and native worker. No bake/loader change or rebake is needed. Raw investigation: `/home/osso/.worktrees/logs/stockade-m2-data.jsonl`, generated by `stockade-m2-investigate.py`. RED reproductions in the same directory: `stockade-server-red.log` and `stockade-engine-red.log`.

## Sky instead of the dungeon

Below the dungeon the camera is in no interior group, so portal culling (WebWowViewerCpp and Retail) draws only the exterior group `ext` and whatever its portals reach. Every Stockade group except `ext` is interior (MOGP 0x2000), so the view is sky. This is correct for a camera outside the WMO.

## Endless fall: fixed

The server let an unsupported player fall forever. It now does what TrinityCore does (MovementHandler.cpp:594-612): a living player below `Map::GetMinHeight` takes `DAMAGE_FALL_TO_VOID` for max health, dies through the normal death flow, and is `OutOfBounds` (`PLAYER_FLAGS_IS_OUT_OF_BOUNDS`) until above it again. The minimum is -500 without MFBO or grid (TerrainMgr.cpp:671, GridMap.cpp:549), so the Stockade uses -500; elsewhere it is `GridMap::getMinHeight` over the root ADT's MFBO minimum planes (Eastern Kingdoms tile (38, 40): -1207.38 at (-4500, -3700)). game-server `3d4373b`.
